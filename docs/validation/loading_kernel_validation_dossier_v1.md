# Urban Cybernetics base loading-kernel validation dossier v1

## Technical summary

The base unit-packet loading kernel satisfies the complete internal validation
matrix, the new composed merge/spillback case, and exact deterministic replay.
The de Souza Figure 5 DT1 lane-drop comparison is measured without calibration;
the Equation (6) correction materially reduces the observed L1 outflow and
storage disagreement while preserving conservation, FIFO, packet identity,
counts, and replay. Within the boundary defined in
`loading_kernel_freeze_v1.md`, the kernel can now be marked frozen.

This is not a claim about OSM import, network-scale performance, routing or
behavioural validity, observability experiments, governance, optimisation, or
future cybernetic mechanisms. A further external de Souza merge or diverge
case is reserved but blocked until its exact source figure, declarations, and
digitised data are supplied.

The closure gate reports 653 Python tests passed with zero failures and 88
subtests passed in 14.87 seconds (15.16 seconds wall time). The frontend suite
reports 26 tests passed with zero failures in 0.453 seconds (0.94 seconds wall

## The composed case demonstrates congestion propagation and clearance

`M8-COMP-NET-01` is a deterministic four-link network. L1 and L2 carry four
packets each on two distinct OD routes, merge with equal declared priority into
L3, and then traverse the lower-rate L4 bottleneck. Link parameters are
triangular-FD consistent and statically parity-eligible.

At tick 3, L3 is full at four packets and queues are simultaneously active on
L1→L3, L2→L3, and L3→L4. All three queues later clear; all eight packets are
complete at tick 10. Tests require exact storage/queue series, storage bounds,
physical eligibility, cumulative entry/exit totals, per-link FIFO, route
lifecycles, conservation, cache consistency, and identical canonical events on
replay. The case and tests are at
`src/urban_cybernetics/canonical_validation/composition.py` and
`tests/validation/test_composed_loading_kernel_case.py`.

## Equation (6) diagnosis and correction are isolated and reproducible

The read-only DT1 audit found the first receiving-credit divergence at the
tick-2 unused-supply case. The old recurrence reduced credit by capacity
offered, thereby discarding unused whole-packet credit. The corrected
recurrence:

1. exposes `floor(previous_credit)` as the integer receiving budget;
2. subtracts actual transferred packets rather than offered capacity;
3. retains unused whole-packet credit;
4. caps credit at `ceil(C * dt) + 1`.

The focused regressions assert that L2 offers zero packets and retains 1.0
packet of credit at tick 2, and that P2 enters L2 at tick 7. The only related
internal oracle update is M8-NODE-01-REOPEN: retained credit exposes three
packets of supply on reopening while the same two packets transfer in FIFO
order. No routing, demand, packetisation, movement, observation, replay, or
event-order rule changed.

## Limitations and robustness boundary

- Unit packets and integer observation ticks remain modeling conventions.
- Figure digitisation introduces coordinate, sampling-phase, duplicate-time,
  and sparse-segment uncertainty.
- Paper-silent packet release, initial credit, and within-tick ordering
  conventions can affect cross-model traces.
- Internal node-family evidence is not an external merge/diverge reproduction.
- DT3 and DT6 Figure 5 runs have complete UC evidence but no supplied published
  curve comparison; DT6 is statically ineligible under the paper's CFL rule.
- Weighted packets, rerouting, adaptive signals, lane changing, gap acceptance,
  and roundabout-specific solvers are outside the frozen kernel claim.
- Existing scale/readiness artifacts do not validate large-network performance
  for this milestone.

## Milestone boundary and next evidence

Validated and frozen: base packetised LTM loading, sending/receiving, storage,
spillback, FIFO, node allocation, canonical events/replay, cumulative counts,
and conservation.

Not validated here: OSM import, large-network performance, routing policy,
behaviour, observability experiments, governance, optimisation, and cybernetic
extensions. They require focused evidence when introduced and may not silently
alter the frozen semantics.

The next external node case requires the supplied de Souza figure identifier
or page, its complete declared parameters and conventions, and digitised
reference data. Once supplied, the existing comparison method can be reused
without calibration. Until then, no result or parameter is inferred.
